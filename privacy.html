Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you book a service or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name — to identify you and your service record
  • Phone number — to contact you about your appointment and send SMS notifications (with your consent)
  • Email address — to send receipts and service confirmations
  • Service address — to dispatch a technician to your location
  • Service details — description of the issue and work performed

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Schedule and complete your service appointment
  • Send appointment reminders and technician dispatch notifications via SMS (only if you have opted in)
  • Send invoices and payment receipts
  • Respond to questions or support requests
  • Improve our services

3. SMS / Text Message Communications

We will only send you SMS messages if you have explicitly opted in by checking the SMS consent checkbox on our booking form.

SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or used for marketing purposes unrelated to your active service appointment.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. You will be immediately unsubscribed and will receive a confirmation. Reply START to re-subscribe. Reply HELP for support.

Message and data rates may apply.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service technicians — dispatched technicians receive your name, address, and service details to complete your job
  • Payment processors — if you pay by card, your payment is processed by a PCI-compliant payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your service records for up to 3 years for warranty and business purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the information below.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us directly for sensitive matters.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes.
